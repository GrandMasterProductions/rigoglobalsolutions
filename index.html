About me

Hi, I’m Rigo. One partner for everything your vision needs.

Most people have to hire a designer, a web developer, a writer and someone who understands paperwork — and then make them all agree. I bring it under one roof.

I’m a designer and builder at heart. I create brands, flyers, presentations and websites, and I’ve built my own businesses and apps — so I know what it takes to go from an idea on paper to something people trust and pay for.

I work with entrepreneurs, families and organizations in Haiti and across the diaspora, in English, Kreyòl and French. Whether you’re launching a company, promoting an event, or organizing important documents, you get a clear plan, a fair price and work finished to a high standard.

— Rigo
